## The fourth blind spot in a guard hardened three times

Four mutations were planted. Three reddened immediately. The fourth —
`_parse_task_table` resolving its header by the old second rule — came up
**green**, and that is the finding:

```
SECOND_RULE = r"=\s*\[[^\]]*?\.lower\(\)[^\]]*?\bfor\b\s+\w+\s+in\s+…"
live shape:  header = ([c.strip().lower() for c in split_row(prev)]
```

One leading paren between `=` and `[`. The comment block above that pattern
records three previous rounds of hardening, all of them **spelling**-shaped
(`for c in cells` caught, `for h in header` not). This one is **shape**-shaped
and walked past all three. Widened to `=\s*[(\[\s]*\[`, re-planted, red.

The lesson belongs on TASK-050: the fifth round should be a mutation harness,
not another regex.
