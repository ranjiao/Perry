## The fourth file, and why it was necessary rather than scope creep

Adding the two claims alone took lint warnings from 3 to **5**. A `.jsonl`
carries no heading, no `files[]` name and no `Owner**:` line, so every rule in
`looks_like_perry_state` read Perry's **own** store and event log as foreign
against Perry's own claim — on this repository and on every adopted project.
The claim would have been self-defeating on the day it landed.

The fix is one helper, `looks_like_perry_record`, called only for `.jsonl`, which
recognises a record **by shape**: a store record is a JSON object with an `id`
and no field outside `perry_store.STORED`, read from the module rather than
respelled; an event carries `ts` and `event`. Recognition is deliberately **not**
by filename — matching the name would excuse the user's own `tasks.jsonl` and
make the claim self-defeating in the other direction.

That is a shape-predicate change in `bin/perry-lint`, outside the declared
`Files in scope`, and the agent flagged it as wanting a reviewer's eye rather
than burying it. `check_ns_collisions` and its wiring from PR #9 are untouched,
so the out-of-scope "emission logic" is unchanged.
