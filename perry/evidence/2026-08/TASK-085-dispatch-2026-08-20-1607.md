## The naming decision, and why it is defensible

`proposed`, not `draft`. The collision with `prescription_status.proposed` is an
argument **for** reuse: both mean an agent-authored artifact awaiting a human's
accept, values are namespaced by their enum, and this schema already reuses
`superseded` across two enums, `dropped` across four and `active` across three.
`draft` would have been the real collision — `design_status.draft` means *still
being written*, and here the drafting is finished and the waiting is the state.
Pinned by a test asserting `proposed` is in the enum and `draft` is not, so the
alternative reads as a decision rather than an accident.

## One judgement call beyond the spec's letter

Left alone, a `proposed` ADR would have rendered under the index's
`## Superseded / Expired / Archived (historical)` heading — a falsehood the
change itself would have introduced. The agent added a
`## Proposed (awaiting the user)` section rendered **only when the project has
one**, so an index with no proposal is byte-identical to before. That preserves
the spec's "available, not mandatory" line rather than breaking it, and it adds
no required heading to the schema.
