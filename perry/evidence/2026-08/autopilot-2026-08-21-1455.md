
Autopilot's stated precondition is *"You have ≥ 3 P0/P1 specs ready to dispatch
(`Dispatch mode: auto`, all dependencies resolved)"*. **That has never been true
on this project**, and the reason is the working pattern rather than any
oversight: the PMO writes a spec **at dispatch time**, for one row, having first
re-verified that row's findings against today's code. The spec and the dispatch
are one act. So a queue of dispatch-ready specs never accumulates for autopilot
to walk.

Three of today's rows show why that pattern earns its cost:

- **TASK-037** — most of its recorded findings had already been fixed by
  TASK-119's rewrite. A spec written in advance would have sent an agent to fix
  three things that no longer existed.
- **TASK-040** — its title still described a markdown-table task that ADR-007
  had made obsolete. Re-scoped at dispatch time.
- **TASK-045** — its precondition (`gate: enforce`) had to be checked at
  dispatch, and its own note carried a constraint that had to reach the prompt
  verbatim or the agent would have deleted fallbacks adoption needs.
