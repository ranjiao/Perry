## Notes

Two things this hand-off found that neither design predicted.

**`parse_yaml_subset` did not support block scalars.** `declarations[].content`
is *specified* as verbatim multi-line — that is the whole reason
`candidates[].resolution` could not hold an authored KR table — but the reader
rejected `key: |` outright, docstring included. Every real dossier would have
failed lint, and `reference/adoption.md § 4` makes a clean lint the gate on
adoption completing. So DESIGN-001's central fix was unshippable, and nothing
surfaced it until TASK-006 created a fixture that used it. The lift had to run
*before* the parser's comment/blank stripping, or a `## Objective 1` heading
inside an authored block was silently eaten.

**`claims[]` shipped with 18 paths, not the 16 DESIGN-002 counted.**
`tests/test_claims.py` caught `architecture/` and `incidents/` on its first run,
both written by PMO. The drift guard found drift in the document that specified
it.

Both are recorded in the designs' `## Changes` sections rather than edited into
the locked bodies.
