All five fields above are mandatory. A card without a source is refused, not
written blank: `no done without evidence`, extended to the knowledge layer.
Without that rule the store becomes a farm of confident errors that agents then

`Invalidated by` is the one people skip and the one that matters most. It is
what makes a card revisable instead of accumulating — name the tripwire, not a
date. "upstream schema change on <system>" is a tripwire; "when it feels old"
is not, and `Last verified` already covers the passage of time.

An invalidated card is ARCHIVED WITH ITS REASON, never deleted. The correction
is itself knowledge, and the next person to reach the same wrong conclusion
needs to find the record of it.

`Kind: source-of-truth` is the other card type: it names an external system,
the authoritative way to read it, and what would falsify that. A role's
`May touch § run` may point at one instead of listing commands, which puts the
